# [SHAPE ROTATOR](https://ungoldman.com/shape-rotator/)

Life getting you down?

Calm your nerves with the soothing power of [SHAPE ROTATOR](https://ungoldman.com/shape-rotator/).

## Controls

- Hold W A S D or drag to steer. Hold Q or E to roll it counterclockwise or clockwise. Letting go stops the shape.
- Let go mid-drag to fling it spinning.
- Double tap a key to let it spin that way. Diagonals count. Tap again to speed it up.
- Zoom with the wheel, a trackpad pinch, two fingers, or + and −.
- Step through the shapes with [ and ], or pick one from the shape menu. Everyday shapes come first, simplest first, from a flat triangle to a geodesic sphere. The esoteric ones follow: hypercube, 16-cell, Möbius strip, trefoil knot, Enneper surface, Klein bottle, double torus.
- Pick an effect from the effect menu or step through them with , and ., held to cycle: mixed ops, solid, wireframe, or any one of the 26 canvas composite operations.
- Reroll the shape, colors, effect, and spin direction with R. Hold it to cycle. Each load starts from a fresh roll.
- Pause with Space.
- Toggle lights with L.
- Open the panel with I.

On a phone the menus and buttons sit at the bottom of the screen.
